  FAMILY CHAT 0.0.0-test      CAN'T OPEN YOUR DATA




    Family Chat can't open your data

    Family Chat's data on this computer is encrypted, and its key is no longer
    in the keychain (after a system reinstall, a keychain reset, or copying
    the data from another computer), so nobody can read it. Start over moves
    it aside, in case the key turns up, and you sign in again. Your messages
    are safe on your homeserver; to read encrypted messages again you need
    your recovery key or another signed-in device.

    Start over keeps the old data in a folder beside the new one, in case the
    key turns up again. Press s to start over.






s StartOvr   q Quit
